Lab(04)

Experiments in interaction that live on this site. They’re here because I’m fascinated by immersive web experiences, and the best way to understand them is to play with them.

Best with a mouse or trackpad. Touch works too.

Particles-- fps

Field Type

Thousands of particles hold the shape of a word. Move through them to scatter the letters, press to push harder and click to change the word.

Optics-- fps

Aperture

The camera diaphragm behind every page transition on this site. Stop down to f/22 or open up to f/1.4, or drag up and down on the lens.

Shutter for equal exposure: 1/1000 s Depth of field: Shallow
Light painting-- fps

Long Exposure

Draw with light the way a camera sees it with the shutter held open. Trails fade slowly. Leave it alone and a ghost painter takes over.

Interference-- fps

Moiré

Two identical ring fields drawn on top of each other. Slide one across the other and large ghost patterns appear that neither field contains.
