Profile

I’m Syed Arham Ashraf, an aspiring entrepreneur, digital creator and technology enthusiast based in Raebareli, Uttar Pradesh, India. I’m the Founder & CEO of QLive.

I’m drawn to the place where technology, business, creativity and digital experiences meet. I don’t see myself as someone who simply builds websites. I look for opportunities, develop ideas, create digital products and turn concepts into something tangible.

I’m still early in my professional journey, and I don’t claim decades of experience or achievements I haven’t earned. What I do have is curiosity, initiative, a willingness to experiment and a genuine drive to build.

Then · Now · Next

From Raebareli, outward

  1. Then

    QLive began as a queue management system for a clinic: token generation, live queue tracking, estimated waiting times and analytics, built with PHP and MySQL.

  2. Now

    Building and exploring across QLive, Nexora, Nuviistudios and Dead Air: business, digital presence, immersive web and game worlds.

  3. Next

    Taking the work from Raebareli into Lucknow and beyond.
