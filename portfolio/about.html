Profile

I’m a creative technologist and interaction designer based in Lisbon. For twelve years I’ve worked between design studios, research labs and engineering teams, usually in the gap where an idea needs both a designer’s eye and a working prototype.

Today I run a small independent practice and take on a handful of projects each year: brands launching something new, museums and observatories explaining something complex, and product teams that want their software to feel alive.

I design in the browser from the first week, write my own shaders and stay through launch, so what ships is what we designed.

Principles

Three rules I keep

Light over noise

Every effect has to earn its place by showing something. If an animation doesn’t help someone understand or feel the work, it goes.

Motion with meaning

Things move the way they would in the world: with weight, momentum and a reason. Timing is designed, never left at a default.

Built to sixty frames

Performance is part of the design. I set a frame budget on day one and test on the oldest phone in the room.
